About

Mitsutaka Tsuryu is a Japan-based fine art and street photographer whose work explores the tension between presence and absence in urban spaces. Through minimal compositions shaped by light, shadow and architectural structure, he captures moments where human presence is suggested rather than directly shown.

His images are quiet and contemplative, inviting a slower attention to the city and what passes through it.

“I hope people feel a sense of calm from my work, as if they have discovered silence hidden inside the city’s noise.”
Mitsutaka Tsuryu
