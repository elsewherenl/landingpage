About

Andrea Rosselli is an Italian photographer working exclusively in analogue and developing his own prints by hand.

His influences span photography, cinema, music, and art, with photographers including Luigi Ghirri, Mario Giacomelli, André Kertész, and Masao Yamamoto informing his approach.

“Working in analogue keeps me connected to the materials and allows the process to remain as artisanal as possible.”
Andrea Rosselli
