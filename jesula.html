Jésula Toussaint Visser

Groningen, The Netherlands

Self-portraits as acts of becoming.

About

Her self-portraits aren’t really about documenting what she looks like; they’re about identity as something unfinished. Photography becomes a way of exploring who she is and who she might become.

Photography is a form of self-expression for Jésula. She invites us to read her work as a visual diary, looking towards who she might become while holding traces of who she has been.

“Most of my own work, including this one, are self portraits that are made about either the concept or the act of being. When I was younger I was immensely busy with what/who I was and could be. Inevitable, as it’s a part of becoming an adult, but I struggled with this quite a bit. Hence why I started photographing as an attempt at autonomy. Later this gradually translated into ‘documents of a she and who she could be.’”

Jésula Toussaint Visser
